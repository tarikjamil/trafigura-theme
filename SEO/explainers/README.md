Rules used:

- Facts come only from trafigurafoundation.org pages (source listed under each draft). Anything that needs a figure the site does not publish is marked **[client to confirm]**.
- Answer first: the opening paragraph answers the title question in 2–3 sentences, so Google and AI tools can quote it.
- H2s are phrased as the questions people ask.
- Each draft ends with a short FAQ. Do **not** add FAQPage schema on news posts unless the FAQ is visible on the page (it is, if pasted as written).
- Suggested publishing order: 04 (supports the “trafigura zambia” query), 01, 02, 03 — one every two to three weeks.
