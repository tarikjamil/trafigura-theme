**People living in informal settlements are among the most exposed to climate change, and among the least protected.** Poor drainage, housing and services leave them vulnerable to floods, heatwaves and landslides, yet adaptation funding rarely reaches them. Upgrading settlements with residents in charge of the data, plans and money is one of the most direct ways to build urban climate resilience.

## Why are informal settlements so vulnerable?

Africa’s population is urbanising fast. More than half of the urban population in Sub-Saharan Africa lives in slums. These neighbourhoods often have poor infrastructure and public services, which leaves residents exposed to heat, floods, storms and landslides.

## Why doesn’t adaptation funding reach them?

Adaptation funding rarely targets informal settlements. Where projects do exist, they are often held back by weak institutional capacity and planning, and residents are left out of decisions about their own neighbourhoods.

## What does community-led upgrading look like?

[Slum Dwellers International (SDI)](/partner-stories/slum-dwellers-international-sdi/) is a global movement with more than 30 years of experience organising urban poor communities. Its model is community-led and data-driven:

1. **Residents collect the data.** Federation members profile their settlements and assess vulnerability, and that evidence feeds into city planning.
2. **Communities pool their savings.** Community-managed finance schemes combine residents’ savings with donor funding.
3. **Upgrades are future-proofed.** Projects include nature-based solutions and access to basic services such as waste management, housing and sanitation.

SDI’s Secretariat is in South Africa and its network supports 22 country affiliates, mainly in Africa.

## How does the Trafigura Foundation support it?

With the Foundation and other funders, SDI is planning, designing, funding and delivering climate-resilient slum upgrading in eight cities in Zambia, Zimbabwe and South Africa, where 4 million people live in slums. The partnership aims for:

- 2,400 federation members trained to run slum profiling and vulnerability assessments
- 400 slum upgrading projects, funded by community-managed finance schemes
- 50,000 slum dwellers with improved resilience to climate change
- 3 national organisations with stronger finances, governance and fundraising

## Frequently asked questions

**What is an informal settlement?**
A neighbourhood built without formal planning or secure tenure, usually with poor infrastructure and services. **[client to confirm wording]**

**Why is slum upgrading climate adaptation?**
Better drainage, housing, sanitation and green space directly reduce the damage from floods, heat and storms.

**Who decides what gets upgraded?**
In SDI’s model, residents do: they collect the data, set priorities and co-manage the finance.
