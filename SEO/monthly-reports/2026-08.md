## 1. Executive summary

August was a **foundation month**: major **technical SEO and performance work** shipped mid-month, while public traffic stayed **seasonally steady** with a slight dip in search clicks (−5% MoM) against a quieter summer period.

**Biggest win:** Homepage mobile Lighthouse **Performance 60 → 99** and **LCP 8.7s → 2.0s**, plus crawl/IA cleanup, unique Yoast metas across the sitemap, and theme-level SEO guards (redirects, alts, share URLs, H1 casing).

**Search snapshot:** **612** Google Search clicks and **50.9K** impressions; average position improved to **8.3** (+7% vs July). Traffic remains **brand-led** (259 brand vs 40 non-brand clicks). **917** GA4 organic sessions (~34% of 2.7K total sessions).

**Focus for September:** Clear stale Facebook/OG descriptions; unique Yoast fields for August’s new content; complete news→partner internal links; Tales index/H1 decision; first formal **AEO prompt spot-check**.

**Client ask:** Confirm whether Tales of Resilience should stay noindex; approve clearing Facebook description overrides in Yoast on hubs and high-traffic pages.
