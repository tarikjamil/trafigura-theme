<!-- 00 Goal -->
<div class="report-section">

<div class="section-header">
<span class="section-num">00</span>
<p class="section-title">Goal of this report</p>
</div>

<p>This monthly report tells the story of how trafigurafoundation.org performed in August — and what we did to strengthen it.</p>

<p>We focus on four questions:</p>

<ul>
<li><strong>What did we deliver?</strong> — the work completed on the site</li>
<li><strong>What went live?</strong> — new stories and partner pages published</li>
<li><strong>How did people respond?</strong> — search visibility, visits, and how the Foundation appears in AI answers</li>
<li><strong>What comes next?</strong> — priorities for September</li>
</ul>

<p>August was a <strong>foundation-building month</strong>: we prioritised site speed, clearer page information for Google, and a cleaner structure — so every visitor and every future piece of content starts from a stronger base.</p>

</div>

<!-- 01 Work completed -->
<div class="report-section">

<div class="section-header">
<span class="section-num">01</span>
<p class="section-title">Work completed</p>
<p class="section-source">Delivery · August 2026</p>
</div>

<div class="bullet-block">
<p>Site performance & technical SEO</p>
<ul>
<li>Homepage speed overhaul (mobile load time 8.7s → 2.0s)</li>
<li>Self-hosted fonts; analytics deferred so the page loads first</li>
<li>Fixed archive and redirect issues; cleaned junk URLs from search</li>
<li>Image descriptions added site-wide; social sharing links corrected</li>
<li>Unique SEO titles and descriptions for all <strong>114</strong> indexed pages</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="bullet-block">
<p>Structure & linking</p>
<ul>
<li>Partner URL clean-up with redirects where slugs were simplified</li>
<li>Stronger internal links on homepage and hub pages</li>
<li>Hub and archive paths kept clean for Google and visitors</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="bullet-block">
<p>Reporting & process</p>
<ul>
<li>Performance dashboard updated (top searches, brand split, organic visits)</li>
<li>Monthly reporting process established</li>
<li>Sitemap baseline saved so we can track content changes every month</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="insight keep-together">
<span class="insight-icon" aria-hidden="true">i</span>
<p>Most of August’s effort was invisible to visitors — but they feel it as a faster homepage, clearer titles in Google, and pages that share correctly on LinkedIn and Facebook. This is the groundwork that future content and campaigns build on.</p>
</div>

<div class="subsection keep-together">
<h3>Top pages from Google</h3>

| Areas of Work | 11 | Pillar hub gaining visibility |

</div>

<div class="insight keep-together">
<p class="insight-title">What this means</p>
<p>Think of <strong>impressions</strong> as how often your site appeared in Google, and <strong>clicks</strong> as how many times someone visited. Fewer impressions in August likely reflects seasonal search behaviour — not a ranking problem. When you do appear, people click more often (CTR up 6% MoM and <strong>+18% vs last August</strong>) and average ranking improved.</p>
<p><strong>Year-on-year:</strong> clicks are essentially flat (+1.2%) versus August 2025 — a healthier signal than the soft MoM dip alone. Most search clicks (259 of 612) still come from people already looking for Trafigura by name; non-brand discovery (40 clicks) is the longer-term growth lever.</p>
</div>

<div class="insight keep-together">
<p class="insight-title">What this means</p>
<p>Overall visits rose slightly MoM (+4.5%) even though Google search visits were flat — usually meaning more people arrived via direct links, email, or referrals. Organic search still represents about <strong>one third of all visits</strong> (917 of 2,700).</p>
<p><strong>Year-on-year:</strong> sessions are nearly flat (−3%), while engagement rate and time on site are softer than August 2025. That is useful context: MoM looked stable-to-up, but the longer view says we should keep improving content depth and discovery — not only celebrate summer MoM noise. Notable movers MoM: Partners Stories hub (+33%), The HALO Trust, Who We Are (+16%).</p>
</div>

<!-- 06 Technical -->
<div class="report-section">

<div class="section-header">
<span class="section-num">06</span>
<p class="section-title">Site speed & technical health</p>
<p class="section-source">Homepage lab test · Before ~12 Aug vs after 14 Aug</p>
</div>

<div class="keep-together table-block">

| Measure | Before | After |
|---|---:|---:|
| Mobile performance score | 60 / 100 | <span class="positive"><strong>99 / 100</strong></span> |
| Time to main content | 8.7 seconds | <span class="positive"><strong>2.0 seconds</strong></span> |
| SEO score (mobile) | 92 / 100 | <span class="positive"><strong>100 / 100</strong></span> |
| Accessibility score | 85 / 100 | <span class="positive"><strong>96 / 100</strong></span> |
| Desktop performance | ~67 / 100 | <span class="positive"><strong>100 / 100</strong></span> |

</div>

<div class="win keep-together">
<p class="win-title">August highlight</p>
<p>The homepage now loads in about <strong>two seconds</strong> on mobile instead of nearly nine — a major improvement for every visitor, especially on phones and in regions with slower connections.</p>
</div>

<div class="insight keep-together">
<p class="insight-title">What this means</p>
<p>This is August’s <strong>standout technical result</strong>. A nine-second wait is slow enough that many people leave; two seconds meets Google’s “good” threshold and delivers a better first impression. We got there by showing a lightweight image first (instead of a large video on mobile), hosting fonts locally, and loading analytics only after the page is usable.</p>
</div>
