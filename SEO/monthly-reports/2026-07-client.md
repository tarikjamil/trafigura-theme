<div class="section-header">
<span class="section-num">00</span>
<p class="section-title">Goal of this report</p>
</div>

<p>This monthly report tells the story of how trafigurafoundation.org performed in July — and what we will prioritise next.</p>

<p>We focus on four questions:</p>

<ul>
<li><strong>What did we deliver?</strong> — SEO and site preparation underway</li>
<li><strong>How did people respond?</strong> — search visibility, visits, and engagement</li>
<li><strong>Where does the site stand technically?</strong> — speed and crawl health</li>
<li><strong>What comes next?</strong> — priorities for August</li>
</ul>

<p>July was a <strong>steady summer month</strong> for traffic, with search still strongly brand-led. Behind the scenes, work continues on performance and technical SEO foundations for the weeks ahead.</p>

</div>

</div>

<div class="insight keep-together">
<p class="insight-title">What this means</p>
<p>July search was <strong>slightly softer than June</strong> on clicks and impressions (typical summer pattern), but CTR rose and average position improved to <strong>7.7</strong>. Demand stayed brand-heavy (305 brand vs 48 non-brand clicks).</p>
<p><strong>Year-on-year:</strong> clicks (−37%) and CTR (−29%) are clearly softer than July 2025, even though position looks stronger. MoM alone would understate the gap — YoY is the signal that content discovery and non-brand visibility need sustained work into autumn.</p>
</div>

<div class="report-section">

<div class="section-header">
<span class="section-num">03</span>
<p class="section-title">What visitors do on the site</p>
<p class="section-source">Google Analytics 4 · July 2026 vs June 2026</p>
</div>

<div class="keep-together table-block">

| Measure | July | Δ MoM |
|---|---:|---|
| Total visits (sessions) | 2,600 | <span class="negative">−21.8%</span> |

</div>

<div class="subsection keep-together">
<h3>Compared to July 2025 (same month last year)</h3>

| Measure | July 2026 | Δ YoY |
|---|---:|---|
| Total visits (sessions) | 2,600 | <span class="negative">−68.3%</span> |
| Visits from Google search | 924 | <span class="negative">−48.9%</span> |
| Engagement rate | 44.8% | <span class="negative">−24.7%</span> |
| Engaged visits | 1,200 | <span class="negative">−76.2%</span> |
| Views | 4,100 | <span class="negative">−69.9%</span> |
| Avg. session duration | 3:50 | <span class="negative">−14.3%</span> |

</div>

<div class="insight keep-together">
<p class="insight-title">What this means</p>
<p>Sessions and views fell versus June, while <strong>time on site rose</strong> and engagement rate held around 45%. That often means fewer but more attentive visits in a quieter period. Organic search still contributed about <strong>one third</strong> of sessions (924 of 2,600).</p>
<p><strong>Year-on-year:</strong> July 2026 is much softer than July 2025 on sessions and views. Treat large YoY gaps carefully (tracking changes, one-off campaigns last year, or measurement differences can inflate the drop) — but the direction is clear enough to keep recovery and content discovery as priorities, not only month-to-month seasonality.</p>
</div>
