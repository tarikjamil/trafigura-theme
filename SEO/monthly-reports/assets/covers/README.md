# Cover photos

Use a **different** cover photo every month. Pick one not in the log below, add the month, and point `.cover-photo` in `YYYY-MM-client.md` at `assets/covers/<file>`.

Landscape works (the cover crops to ~1.7:1, `object-position: center 30%`). Prefer photos from the Foundation’s own site; ideally one tied to that month’s content.
